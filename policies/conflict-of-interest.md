**Status:** adopted (Article X of the bylaws) · **Owner:** the Board · **Not legal advice**

## <img src="../assets/icons/compass.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Where the policy lives
PIR®'s conflict-of-interest policy is adopted as **Article X of the bylaws**, and its full text is public in PIR®'s
profile repository: [`bylaws/conflict-of-interest.md`](https://github.com/psychedelicsinrecovery/.github/blob/main/bylaws/conflict-of-interest.md).
It is based on the IRS model policy attached to Form 1023. This file points there instead of keeping a second copy, so
the two can never disagree.

## <img src="../assets/icons/calendar-days.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> What it asks each year
Each director signs an annual conflict-of-interest disclosure ([compliance requirements](https://github.com/psychedelicsinrecovery/.github/blob/main/bylaws/compliance-requirements.md)).
The signed forms are kept in `entity/`, never in this folder and never in a public lane.
